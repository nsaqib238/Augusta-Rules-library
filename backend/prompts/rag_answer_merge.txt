Field guidance:
- Use conclusion "depends" when clauses state requirements but site-specific facts or table lookups are needed.
- **required_tables**: list every table the user must consult or supply inputs for. Be specific about table_ref and information_needed (e.g. cable size, IP rating, building class). Use when conclusion is insufficient_evidence OR depends and a table lookup is the blocker.
- **missing_clause_refs**: clause numbers referenced in the question or gaps but absent from retrieved evidence.
- "short_answer": plain English summary, not a list of clause numbers alone.
- Include every materially cited clause/table in the citations array with clause_id and claim.
