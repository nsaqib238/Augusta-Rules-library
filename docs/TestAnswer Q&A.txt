Q1 — What are the requirements for a building occupant warning system under the NCC?
================================================================================
Time: 13:51
Confidence: 90%
Evidence blocks: 32 (evidence-bound)
Result: PASS

Top citations: S17C8, S20C7 (+ S31C19, J6D4 in supporting pool)

--- Answer (summary) ---

Practitioner summary
Outcome: Based only on the cited clauses - apply the requirements below to the installation and use any verification items as applicable.

A building occupant warning system must comply with specific requirements depending on the building's class and use. It should be connected to a required sprinkler system and activate upon detection of smoke. The system must comply with AS 1670.1 and provide adequate sound levels, with specific adjustments allowed for certain building classes to minimize trauma.

What the cited clauses require
- A building occupant warning system must comply with clause 3.22 of AS 1670.1.
- The system must sound through all occupied areas unless specific exceptions apply.
- In Class 2 and 3 buildings or Class 4 parts, sound pressure levels need not be measured within sole-occupancy units if certain conditions are met.
- In Class 3 buildings used as residential care, the system must provide a warning and may adjust alarm volume and content.
- In Class 9a health-care buildings, the system must provide a warning and may adjust alarm volume and content in ward areas.
- In Class 9c buildings, the system must notify staff and may adjust alarm volume and content in resident areas.

Exceptions, notes, and edge cases
- Class 2/3/4 with smoke alarm: not less than 85 dB(A) at access door.
- Class 2/3/4 with smoke detection: not less than 100 dB(A) at access door.
- Class 3 residential care / 9a ward / 9c resident areas: alarm may be adjusted to minimize trauma.

Citations
- S17C8 - A required sprinkler system must be connected to and activate a building occupant warning system.
- S20C7 - AS 1670.1 cl 3.22; sound pressure levels and class-specific adjustments.

Evaluator notes
- Strong pass; added S17C8 (sprinkler linkage) vs prior run (S20C7 only).
- Note: sprinkler connection applies when sprinkler is required — not universal for all buildings; confirm class/triggers on site.
- Still describes HOW to comply more than WHEN system is required (acceptable for this question wording).
