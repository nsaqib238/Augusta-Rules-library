Sample · AS/NZS 3000

Electrical Q&A — mixed circuits

Question

What are the rules associated with mixed circuits?

Document: AS3000 2018 · Confidence 90% · 31 evidence blocks

Practitioner summary
Rules for mixed circuits depend on circuit type and voltage levels. Different voltages need protection against faults via segregation or overvoltage protection. SELV and PELV have specific separation requirements.

What the cited clauses require

  • Protection against harmful influence between different circuits (1.5.11.5)
  • Segregation or overvoltage protection for different voltages (1.5.11.2)
  • SELV/PELV separated from higher voltage circuits (7.5.4)

Sample · NCC 2022 Vol 1

NCC Q&A — emergency lighting (Class 6)

Question

What is the requirement of emergency light in a Class 6 building?

NCC 2022 — Vol 1 — All · Confidence 90% · Codes: ncc2022_vol1

Practitioner summary
Emergency lighting is required in specific areas: fire-isolated stairways, passageways, storeys >300 m², rooms >100 m² without direct exit access, and public-access spaces per layout rules.

What the cited clauses require

  • Emergency lighting in every fire-isolated stairway, passageway, or ramp
  • Every storey >300 m² in a Class 6 building
  • Rooms >100 m² or >300 m² per access path rules
  • Compliance with AS/NZS 2293.1 for emergency lighting systems

Supporting NCC provisions

E4D2 · SA E4D2 · E4D4 · E4D5 · F6D2 · D4D2 · … (+25 more)

Sample · Victorian SIR 2025

SIR Q&A — supply capacity overload

Question

What is the rule about overloading the supply capacity device for short periods?

SIR: Victorian SIR 2025 · Confidence 70%

Practitioner summary
Overloading is permitted for short periods: installations shall not exceed 130% of the Distributor's nominated supply capacity for more than 2 hours, with specific circuit breaker thermal settings per IEC/AS 60947 or 60898.

Key citation: 6.7.1.2
